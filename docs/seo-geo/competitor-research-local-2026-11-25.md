The user reports these brands appeared in signed-out ChatGPT search answers while Dr. Shabnam did not. Those answers, exact prompts and cited URLs were not supplied for independent inspection here. This audit establishes public content differences, not which sources ChatGPT used or why it selected a brand. Mention, citation and recommendation are different observations. No ranking, indexing or citation benefit is promised.

## Blossom identification: sufficiently corroborated local official site

**Best-supported official local page:** <https://www.blossomfc.com/>; contact page: <https://www.blossomfc.com/contact-us/>. The homepage identifies Blossom Fertility Centre on the second floor of A.G. Padmavati's Hospital, Villianur Main Road, Arumparthapuram, Puducherry 605110. Its contact page gives the same locality and public contact identity. [B1, B2]

The government's National ART & Surrogacy Registry lists **BLOSSOM FERTILITY CENTRE**, **127/1A, II Floor, Villianur Main Road, Arumparthapuram, Puducherry**, registration **ART-PY-09**, with the same public email as the website. Matching brand, floor, road, locality and email support the domain-to-local-clinic association. The site homepage does not display that street number in the fetched text; retain that distinction. [R1]

This is public identity corroboration, not proof of domain ownership, current certificate validity, facilities, outcomes or service quality. Other Blossom businesses exist: do not merge the Puducherry entity with similarly named clinics elsewhere. This audit did not inspect its certificate or regulatory status beyond the listing. [R1]

An important registry ambiguity: the separately indexed **rejected-clinic** list also includes a Blossom entry at the same street/floor/locality, with a different email, a Tamil Nadu state label and application **TN/AC/2022/12769**. The relationship and chronology between that application and ART-PY-09 are unresolved. Do not infer either present invalidity or clean current registration from these two search excerpts. The registered-list email/address match still supports local website identification; regulatory verification requires the actual records. [R2]

## Recommendations under the no-visible-change constraint

### Immediately feasible without changing UI, visible copy or navigation

1. **Preserve and monitor the implemented foundations.** The supplied current baseline is SSR, connected MedicalClinic/WebSite/WebPage JSON-LD, canonical, wildcard robots, homepage sitemap and GSC indexing. Local `clinic.js`, `routes.js`, `news.js` and `index.html` confirm the schema relationships, homepage-only routes and existing source links; GSC indexing is user-reported, not independently rechecked. Continue existing HTTP/schema parity checks. Do not propose SSR or basic schema as missing competitor advantages.
2. **Create a reproducible observation record before optimization.** For each permitted signed-out search run, retain exact query, date, language/location conditions, answer citations, owned-domain citation versus mere mention, and repeated-run counts. Keep unavailable results unknown. This is research/documentation work; no account changes are needed. The current anecdote cannot establish a visibility trend.
3. **Audit existing machine-readable identity against already approved public facts.** Check stable IDs, canonical consistency and source-backed metadata during regression review. This research demonstrates no specific missing tag requiring a change. Do not add ratings, outcomes, credentials, hours or hidden FAQ text to imitate competitor breadth. Validate any proposed schema enrichment against approved visible content and evidence first.

### Owner proof, external authority or a separate visible-content scope required

1. **Reconcile clinic identity and care locations first.** Resolve the existing proof ledger's name/contact/registry questions and distinguish Nellithope consultations from any associated procedure facilities. Then prepare an owner-reviewed citation correction queue. Directory/GBP edits require account authority and accurate public facts; none were made here.
2. **If visible-content work is later approved, address decision questions.** A substantial local treatment page could cover assessment, alternatives, visit sequence, who performs each stage and where, estimate inclusions/exclusions, risks and uncertainty, and appointment confirmation. ARC and Srishti show these topic types; their medical wording and claims are not reusable evidence. The existing draft IVF/doctor/contact pages must remain unpublished under the current scope.
3. **Verify clinician/team and educational attribution.** Obtain actual qualifications, registration evidence, procedure responsibilities and clinician sign-off before profile or reviewer additions. Blossom illustrates explicit team roles and an educational source link, not a reason to invent a reviewer badge. Existing NHS/HFEA Patient Resources can remain as implemented.
4. **Earn accurate external references through real activity.** Correct legitimate owner-authorized profiles and document genuine affiliations or public educational work when they occur. Srishti's acquisition coverage reflects an actual corporate event; it is not a template for manufactured press, purchased endorsements or fabricated partnerships. No outreach is authorized by this audit.
